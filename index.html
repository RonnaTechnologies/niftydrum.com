How It Works

From pad to MIDI in 5 steps.

NiftyDrum delivers a complete path from piezo hit to sound — wire it, map it, play it.

1

Wire up

Connect up to 9 piezos and 1 FSR to the terminal blocks.

2

Plug in

Plug into your DAW or drum module via USB.

3

Shape

Use our GUI for trigger parameters, mapping and velocity shaping.

4

Play

Notes stream out instantly with zero perceivable latency.

5

Unleash

Use the NiftyDrum+ sound engine and get a fully-featured drum module.

More Than Just Drums

A blank canvas for makers.

Thanks to our open, Arduino-compatible SDKs, NiftyDrum+ lets you create a custom drum module, interactive music boxes, or entirely new instruments.

Sensor SDK · STM32F1

Arduino-compatible library for the sensor-to-MIDI interface. Read piezos and FSR, customize trigger logic, and stream MIDI over USB.
