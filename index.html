How It Works

NiftyDrum delivers high-level MIDI performance in 4 or 5 easy steps:

  • Connect up to 9 piezos and 1 FSR to the terminal blocks
  • Plug into your DAW or drum module via USB
  • Use our GUI for trigger parameters, mapping and velocity shaping
  • Play — notes stream out instantly with zero perceivable latency
  • Use NiftyDrum+ sound engine and get a fully-featured drum module

Meet NiftyDrum+

Take your builds to the next level with NiftyDrum+, a single, unified board combining our sensor-to-MIDI interface with a powerful sound engine.

  • All-in-One: Sensors + Sound generation on a single board.
  • Ultra-Low Latency: Total system latency (sensor to audio) is < 10ms.
  • Ready to Play: Comes with a pre-installed drum module firmware.

Technical Specifications

Hardware and performance metrics for the NiftyDrum+ platform.

Hardware

  • Inputs 9 Piezo + 1 FSR
  • Connectors Terminal blocks
  • USB USB 2.0 Type-C
  • Audio TRS 3.5mm (⅛")

Performance

  • MIDI Latency <2.5ms
  • Trigger to Sound <10ms
  • Sampling Rate >10kHz
  • Audio Rate ≥44.1kHz

Software

  • Compatibility Win / Mac / Linux
  • Interface Web-based GUI
  • Firmware USB Updates
  • Development Arduino SDKs
