How It Works

NiftyDrum delivers high-level MIDI performance in 4 easy steps:

  • Connect up to 9 piezos and 1 FSR to the terminal blocks
  • Plug into your DAW or drum module via USB
  • Use our GUI for trigger parameters, mapping and velocity shaping
  • Play — notes stream out instantly with zero perceivable latency

Important: To ensure proper USB device access, you may need to add your user to the dialout group. Run this command in your terminal:

Then log out and log back in for the changes to take effect.
