How It Works

Professional MIDI Performance

NiftyDrum delivers pro-level MIDI performance in 4 easy steps:

  • Connect up to 9 piezos and 1 FSR with standard 1/4" jacks
  • Plug into your DAW or drum module via USB
  • Use our GUI for trigger parameters, mapping and velocity shaping
  • Play — notes stream out instantly with zero perceivable latency
